# Privacy Policy — 0FluffStart

0FluffStart is built from the ground up to be a local-first, privacy-respecting dashboard and browser extension.

### Core Commitments

- **Zero Telemetry:** No analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or remote logging engines are present in this codebase.
- **Device-Only Storage:** All configuration settings, custom links, search history, and background media payloads remain stored strictly on your local device via `localStorage` and `IndexedDB`.
- **No Accounts Required:** You are never required to register, sign in, or transmit personal data to external servers.
